## Project Idea

MedRoute AI is a smart hospital recommendation and emergency navigation system.

Instead of recommending only the nearest hospital, it recommends the most suitable hospital based on:

- emergency type
- required medical specialty
- distance
- estimated travel time
- ICU support
- emergency facilities
- hospital capabilities

---

## Problem Statement

During an emergency, the nearest hospital may not always be the correct hospital.

It may not have:

- the required specialist
- ICU support
- emergency treatment facilities
- suitable equipment
- required medical capabilities

MedRoute AI aims to recommend hospitals based on medical suitability and travel feasibility.
